# ADR-0019: No numeric limits in Community

- **Status:** Accepted (design phase)
- **Date:** 2026-10-27

## Context
Usage caps frustrate the community and slow adoption.

## Decision
Community has unlimited users, projects, runners and deployments. Editions differ by capability only.

## Alternatives considered
Cap users (e.g. 5) or runners/projects.

## Consequences
Revenue depends on governance, compliance and scale features being genuinely valuable.
